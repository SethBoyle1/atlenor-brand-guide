01 · Brand

Calm, precise, in control

Atlenor makes the Atlenor Command Center™: one dashboard over a business's back-end systems. The brand should feel the way the product works.

Calm

Lots of dark space. One light source. Nothing shouts.

Precise

Hand-drawn letters, exact spacing, real numbers. Every detail is deliberate.

In control

The whole operation on one screen. The brand looks like it already has things handled.

02 · Story

One ring. One star.

“Atlenor holds your whole operation together, and shows you which way is north.”

ATL · the ring

From Atlas, who held everything together. The ring is the whole operation, held in one place.

NOR · the star

From the North Star, the fixed point you steer by. It sits at the center of the O.

The opening

The gap at the top of the ring is where the star breaks out and leads the way forward.

A book of maps is also called an atlas, which gives a second meaning: Atlenor maps the business. Tell the story in words only. Never use mythological figures or religious imagery.

03 · Logo

The logo

Semibold ATLENOR, drawn by hand, with the ring and star as its O. Mirror chrome is the signature finish. In real-light images, the starlight stays inside the O, and its spikes end just past the ring.

04 · Anatomy

How it's built

Seven custom-drawn letters. The O is the mark: an open ring with a four-point star at its center, whose top point rises through the opening.

OpeningRing · ATLStar · NORCustom semibold letters

Letters

Semibold, wide and geometric, with angled cuts at the ends of strokes. They are not a font.

Ring

About 8% of the O's width. It opens at the top by 18 degrees. The small mark has a thicker ring for small sizes.

Star

Four points with curved sides. The top point is twice as long as the others and passes up through the opening.

05 · Finishes

One logo, six finishes

The shapes never change. Only the finish does. Use mirror chrome unless there's a reason not to.

06 · Real light

The signature images

Photographic renders of the chrome logo lit by its own star. Use them for big moments: the website hero, title slides, video, social headers and the lobby wall. For everything else, use the flat vector logo.

Each image also comes in a pure version, with no blue, in the same folder.

07 · Breakthrough effect

The star breaks through

A special-effects version where the star's light shoots up and out through the opening in the O. It's for launches, video intros, event screens and announcements. It is never the everyday logo.

Logo intro, 4.7 seconds: the star ignites, the chrome lights up, and the beam breaks out north. Also included as a GIF.

When to use it

  • Product launches and announcements
  • Video intros and outros
  • Event screens and trade-show walls
  • Story and reel covers

When not to

  • Website header, app icon, documents and apparel. Use the standard logo.
  • Next to the standard logo on the same screen.
  • Below 600 pixels wide.

08 · Space and size

Give it room

Rules

  • Clear space on every side: half the height of the O.
  • Smallest full logo: 120 pixels wide on screen, 1 inch in print, 2.5 inches in embroidery.
  • Smallest mark: 24 pixels. Below 32 pixels, use the small mark, which has a thicker ring.
  • Real-light images: at least 600 pixels wide.
  • Embroidery, engraving and foil: flat one- or two-color files only, never gradients.

10 · Color

Black, chrome and baby blue

Black is the base of everything. Chrome is the signature. Baby blue is the one accent, used sparingly, like the starlight in the O.

Ratio

About 85% black, 12% chrome and text, and 3% baby blue. The darkness is what makes the chrome shine. No gold: warm gold fights the cool chrome.

In print

Chrome on paper is best as metallic silver ink or foil, close to Pantone 877 C. Print black as rich black (C60 M40 Y40 K100) or Pantone Black 6 C. Baby blue is close to Pantone 2905 C. Confirm all three with the printer. Color codes for print, web and code are in 05-color/.

12 · Imagery

One star, one light source

Do

  • One star per image, in the O.
  • Black space and fine star dust.
  • Real screenshots of the product, on black.
  • Real photos of real customers and staff, with permission.

Don't

  • Extra stars, lens flares, planets or galaxies.
  • Mythological figures, globes on shoulders or religious imagery.
  • AI images made to look like real places or people.
  • Stock photos of handshakes and people pointing at screens.

Every star is ours

All starlight and light effects in this kit were generated for Atlenor. Don't add stock light effects.

17 · Apparel

On clothing

Dark garments, white letters, and a metallic silver O. Keep it understated: a left-chest logo or a small mark looks more premium than a giant chest print.

Garment drawings show placement only. Printed tees can use the white or one-color files. Never print the real-light chrome images on fabric.

18 · Embroidery

For the embroidery shop

Threads

  • A, letters: white
  • B, ring and star: metallic silver, close to Pantone 877 C
  • Light garments: black #000000, your standard black thread
  • At 2 inches or smaller, use one thread.

Sizes
